Products

ArmaGear owns and operates a growing family of software products. Each carries its own brand — all are engineered, run and evolved by us.

Company Gear

Live in production

A complete company operating system — people, communication, documents, meetings, tasks and finances in one platform, run by an integrated AI secretary.

Each deployment is configured to the client's own structure and industry. The first Company Gear system is live in production today, running the day-to-day operations of a Dubai professional services firm.

▸AI secretary & chat ▸Meetings & minutes ▸Documents ▸Tasks & workflows ▸Finance & reporting ▸Access control

LexGear OS

Live in production

AI-powered management for modern law firms — built to deliver legal services at a speed that simply wasn't possible before.

LexGear OS weaves artificial intelligence into the day-to-day running of a law firm — working alongside lawyers as an assistant on every case: reading documents, drafting opinions, surfacing what matters. Work that once took days now takes a fraction of the time, and the lawyer's attention goes where it should: strategy, execution and representation.

Smart intake, live cases

A prospective client describes their situation and the case comes alive inside the portal — the legal team picks it up already equipped to draft, refine and examine every related document in the finest detail.

Case management, end to end

Every matter carries its status through the firm's real workflow — inquiry, consultation, signed, execution, closed. Tasks attach to cases and roll into a permanent history; a shared calendar keeps deadlines visible. Each case is a single source of truth.

The commercial engine

Introducers and business developers are tracked against the matters they bring in, with commissions calculated and paid out transparently. Finance is built in — revenue, expenses, overhead and a live profit-and-loss view, without a spreadsheet in sight.

Built around the firm's hierarchy

Partners, legal staff, business developers and support each see exactly what they should — financial data restricted to those entitled, commissions visible to those who earn them. The org chart is encoded in the system, not bolted on beside it.

▸AI case assistant ▸Smart intake ▸Case workflow ▸Commissions & finance ▸Role-based access
